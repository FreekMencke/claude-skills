# Default report

Use this only when neither the caller nor the repo sets a format. Leave out a section with nothing in it, except
"Open questions": write "None" there.

```markdown
# <Question, as a short title>

Researched <YYYY-MM-DD>. <One to three sentences: the answer, or the recommendation and what it rests on.>

## Questions

1. <question>: <one-line answer, or "not found">

## Findings

### <Question 1>

- <Claim>. "<exact quoted wording for any figure>" ([<source title>](<URL>), accessed <YYYY-MM-DD>, <raw text | fetch>;
  <confidence>)
- <Claim, contested>: [<source A>](<URL>) says "<quote>"; [<source B>](<URL>) says "<quote>". <Which to trust and why.>

## Tested

- <What was tested>: `<command>` on <surface>, <version>, <window and sample size>. Result: <result>.

## Refuted or dropped

- <Claim>: <why it was dropped, with the counter-source>.

## Recommendations

1. <Recommendation> (rests on: <claims>)

## Open questions

- <Question>: <where you looked and what was missing>

## Sources

- [<title>](<URL>), accessed <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

Confidence values: official docs, official example, community consensus, single source, unverified.
